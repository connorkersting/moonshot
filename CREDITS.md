Musopen asks (does not require) attribution: thank you, Musopen. Armstrong took Dvořák's
"New World" symphony to the Moon on Apollo 11, which is why it carries this trailer.

**Open rights caveat (Holst).** The USAF Band *recording* is public domain, and Holst's score is
public domain in the US (published 1921). But the band plays a 1998 wind-ensemble transcription by
Merlin Patterson, and that arrangement may itself be under copyright. Commons lists the file as
plain PD. This is fine for private viewing. Before any public release, swap "Mars" for an
orchestral PD recording (e.g. a Musopen or other CC0 performance of the original orchestration)
or confirm the arrangement's status.
